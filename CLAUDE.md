I am developing a liftshare system for a climbing team at university.
Everyone enters their postcode, whether they have a car, and how many seats they have
The system automatically fills the cars such that
1. The number of cars used is minimised
2. The distance people have to walk to the nearest car is minimised
Ultimately, it will be a web app, using real walking travel times rather than straight-line distance.
Handling for  prefilling cars based on friendships before the algorithm is applied.

At the moment, I am just trying to develop a basic system with test data, that fills cars based on straight-line distance between coordinates.
